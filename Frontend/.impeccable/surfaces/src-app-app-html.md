# Empify listening workspace

Scope: responsive desktop and mobile music-library application. Mode: Operate.
Audience and job: inferred personal-music listeners who upload, organize, and
play their own tracks. Content: clearly labeled synthetic demo tracks and
playlists until users connect their own library. Constraint: do not imply a
third-party streaming catalog or a live backend.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A personal library should feel browsable like a well-kept record-shop
crate, while remaining an unmistakably efficient music app.

OWN-WORLD: Use warm inlay paper, ballpoint-blue ink, and TDK red as the label
accent. Hand-lettered display titles carry the mixtape J-card's human touch;
workhorse sans-serif type and compact catalog numbering keep track metadata
scannable. Keep navigation, rows, inputs, and controls familiar to web apps.
The signature move is a red catalog mark that tracks the active song's
progress across its row and repeats that position in the player.

STORY: The visitor sees a private demo library, finds a track or playlist,
plays it, and can upload their own music without confusing samples for real
content.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A cobalt navigation rail anchors the left; a bright, open
workspace holds the page title and upload action, then a compact collection
summary, search, and a readable track table. The active row is marked with its
catalog label and playback line. A persistent bottom player carries transport
controls and track progress; on mobile, navigation condenses and the player
stays reachable without covering the current task.
